# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively. Do
not engage in harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or publication of
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that
violate these standards. Repeated or serious violations may result in a ban from
project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `oliver.braun@nucos.de`. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
